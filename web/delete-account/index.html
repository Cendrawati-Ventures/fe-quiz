Delete Account Request - Quiz QTA

Last updated: 29 April 2026

This page is provided by Rifki Rusdi Satma Putra for users of Quiz QTA who want to request account deletion without reopening the app.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to rifkirusdisatmaputra@gmail.com.
  2. Use subject: Account Deletion Request - Quiz QTA.
  3. Include these details:
    • Registered email address.
    • Full name and user ID (if available).
    • Institution/class name (if applicable).

What Happens Next

  • We verify account ownership to protect against unauthorized deletion.
  • We process valid requests within 7 business days.
  • You will receive confirmation when deletion is completed.

Data Deleted

  • Account profile and login association.
  • Exam session records linked to the account.
  • User preferences and device-session mappings.

Data That May Be Retained Temporarily

Some records may be retained for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, dispute handling, or audit requirements. Retained data is minimized and stored only as long as required by applicable obligations.

In-App Option

If you can still access the app, you may also request deletion from in-app account settings.
