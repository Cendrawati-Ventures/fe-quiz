Delete Account Request - Quiz Bali Q_TA by Nusadex

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This page is provided by Nusadex, the developer and publisher of Quiz Bali Q_TA on Google Play, for users who want to request account deletion without reopening the app.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to rifkirusdisatmaputra@gmail.com.
  2. Use subject: Account Deletion Request - Quiz Bali Q_TA.
  3. Include these details:
    • Registered email address.
    • Full name and user ID (if available).
    • Institution/class name (if applicable).

What Happens Next

  • We verify account ownership to protect against unauthorized deletion.
  • We process valid requests within 7 business days.
  • You will receive confirmation when deletion is completed.

Data Deleted

  • Account profile, login credentials, and class membership.
  • Exam attempts, answers, and results linked to the account.
  • Proctoring events and recordings linked to the account.

Data Retention

Account data has no additional retention period after deletion is completed. Request correspondence and identity-verification details may be retained for up to 30 days after completion, then deleted. Data required by law may be retained only for the legally required period.

In-App Option

If you can still access the app, open the account/profile menu, select Hapus Akun, then confirm Ya, Hapus.
