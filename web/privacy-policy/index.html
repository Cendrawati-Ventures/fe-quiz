1. Data We Collect

We may collect and process the following categories of data:

  • Account data: name, email address, user ID, class or institution details.
  • Exam and proctoring data: exam session metadata, attendance/presence status, anti-cheat events, and media streams from camera and microphone while the proctoring feature is active.
  • Device and app data: app version, OS version, device model, network diagnostics, and technical logs.
  • Crash and performance data: error and stability telemetry to maintain service reliability.

2. Why We Use Data

  • Provide login, account, and exam functionality.
  • Run proctoring and exam integrity controls, including anti-cheat and security monitoring.
  • Detect abuse, fraud, and suspicious activity.
  • Fix bugs, monitor app health, and improve performance.
  • Comply with legal or regulatory obligations.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited data with service providers that process data on our behalf, such as:

  • Realtime communication provider (for proctoring video/audio transport).
  • Cloud and analytics providers (for app operation, diagnostics, and reliability).

These parties are required to use data only for authorized service purposes and to apply reasonable security controls.

4. Data Security

  • Data is encrypted in transit using secure protocols.
  • Access to personal data is restricted based on role and business need.
  • We apply technical and organizational safeguards to reduce unauthorized access risk.

5. Data Retention

We retain data for as long as needed to provide services, maintain exam integrity, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations. Data that is no longer required will be deleted or de-identified.

6. Account Deletion and Data Deletion Requests

If your app account can be created in Quiz Bali Q_TA, you can request account deletion through:

  • In-app deletion flow (inside app settings/profile).
  • Web request page: ../delete-account/

When we process a valid deletion request, we delete the account and associated personal data, except data that must be retained for legal compliance, security, or fraud prevention.

7. Your Rights
